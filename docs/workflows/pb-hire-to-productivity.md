## Inputs

- 📋 Role title and team it belongs to
- 📋 Why the hire is needed (growth, replacement, new capability)
- 💰 Budget / compensation range
- 📅 Timeline (when do you need someone started)
- 📋 Must-have vs nice-to-have skills

## What BOB does

1. team-composition-analysis → define the role: responsibilities, skills needed, reporting line, success criteria
2. hr-ops → create job description, choose sourcing channels, set interview process
3. Review JD and interview plan, begin sourcing
4. linkedin-searches → build Boolean searches for candidate sourcing on LinkedIn
5. Interview candidates, check references, make offer
6. employment-contract-templates → draft offer letter and employment contract

## Outputs

- 📋 Role Definition.md
- 📋 Job Description.md
- 🔍 Boolean Searches.md
- 📄 Offer Letter.pdf
- 🗺️ 30/60/90 Plan.md
- 📋 Review Doc.md
