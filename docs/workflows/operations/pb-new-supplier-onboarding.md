*Supplier profiled, evaluated, contracted, and ready for first PO*

> New supplier.
> No profile. No evaluation. No contract terms documented.

**Outcome:** Supplier profiled, evaluated, contracted, and ready for the first PO.

- **Area:** Operations
- **Time savings:** ~4 hrs manual → ~1 hr automated (4× faster)
- **Try it:** "Decision to engage a new supplier"
- **Compounding:** Supplier profiles and Kraljic data build procurement intelligence
- **Frequency:** Per new supplier
- **Published:** 2026-03-30

## Inputs

- 🏢 Supplier company name and website
- 📦 Services/products to be provided
- 💰 Expected annual spend and terms

## What BOB does

1. Create supplier profile in 20-COMPANY/30-SUPPLIERS/
2. strategic-sourcing → classify supplier using Kraljic matrix
3. strategic-sourcing → generate RFx or negotiation brief
4. Negotiate terms, sign contract, agree pricing and SLA
5. strategic-sourcing → run contract checklist review
6. Update supplier profile with contract terms, prepare for first PO

## Outputs

- 👤 Supplier profile with contacts
- 📊 Kraljic classification
- ✅ Contract checklist review
- 📄 Ready-for-PO supplier record

## Benefits

- 4× faster
- Evaluation scorecard — not gut feel
- Contract terms documented from day one
