> You won the work.
> Now scope, schedule, and handoff all live in your head.

- **Area:** Operations
- **Time savings:** Typical run: Runs across the delivery lifecycle — days to months
- **Try it:** "New project or engagement starts — need to plan, execute, and hand off deliverables"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 4 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per trigger event
- **Published:** 2026-08-01

## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name and project scope
- 📅 Agreed deliverables and timeline
- 💰 Budget or effort allocation
- 📋 Key stakeholders and their roles
- 📋 Quality standards or acceptance criteria

## What BOB does

1. project-context → establish delivery context: scope, constraints, stakeholders, success criteria
2. project-schedule → build delivery timeline with milestones and dependencies
3. Review plan with client, confirm milestones and check-in cadence
4. project-status → generate status updates at each check-in
5. Client success capture → collect testimonial, update health score, identify upsell

## Outputs

- 📋 Project Charter.md
- 📅 Delivery Schedule.md
- 📅 Status Updates.md
- 📋 Handoff Package.md
- 📊 Retention Report.md
- 📊 Health Score.md
