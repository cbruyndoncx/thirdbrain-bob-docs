## What BOB does

1. icp-discovery → define ideal customer profile
2. social-media-audit → assess current social presence
3. brand-voice-generator → extract brand voice guidelines
4. Review ICP, audit findings, and brand voice
5. content-strategy → build topic clusters and calendar
6. social-content → write platform-specific posts
7. email-sequence → create nurture sequences
8. cro → optimize landing pages and conversion paths
