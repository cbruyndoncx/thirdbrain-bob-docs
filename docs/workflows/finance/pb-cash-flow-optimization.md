**Outcome:** Cash flow visibility — before the gap becomes a crisis.

- **Area:** Finance
- **Time savings:** ~8 hrs manual → ~2 hrs automated (4× faster)
- **Try it:** "Client has cash flow gaps or late payments"
- **Compounding:** AR tracking and invoice improvements reduce DSO month over month
- **Frequency:** Per cash flow intervention
- **Published:** 2026-03-26

## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name and industry
- 💳 Average invoice value and payment terms
- 📉 Late payment percentage and cash reserve runway

## What BOB does

1. business-baseline → map cash flow cycle and bottlenecks
2. Review cash flow map — confirm pain points
3. business-reporting → build cash flow analysis with forecasting
4. ar-followup → set up AR tracking and follow-up templates
5. invoice-description → optimize invoice clarity and terms
6. kpi-dashboard-design → design cash flow monitoring dashboard

## Outputs

- 📊 Cash flow analysis with aging report
- 📧 AR follow-up templates
- 🧾 Optimized invoice descriptions
- 📈 Cash flow monitoring dashboard

## Benefits

- Collections tighten automatically
- DSO drops month over month
- No more surprises
