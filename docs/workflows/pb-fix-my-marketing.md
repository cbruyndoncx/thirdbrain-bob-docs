## Inputs

- 🌐 Website URL
- 🌐 Social media profile URLs (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, YouTube)
- 📋 Current marketing spend and channels (if known)
- 🎯 Target audience or ICP description
- 🌐 Key competitors (2-3 names or URLs)
- 📦 Specific pain points or focus areas (optional)

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → verify client context completeness and flag blocking gaps before proceeding
2. product-marketing-context → establish or verify marketing foundation
3. brand-voice-generator → establish or verify brand voice guidelines
4. website-audit → full SEO + GEO site analysis
5. social-media-audit → platform-level social presence audit
6. marketing-report → merge all findings into scored report + PDF
7. Review audit results — prioritize gaps and set focus areas
8. content-strategy → 60-day topic clusters and content calendar from audit gaps
9. social-content → 60-day social posting calendar aligned to strategy
10. Review plan — approve topics and select quick-win pieces to draft
11. seo-content-machine → draft first 3 priority articles from plan
12. content-scorer → quality gate all drafts ≥ 70

## Outputs

- 📊 Marketing Audit.pdf
- 📋 Gap List.md
- ✏️ Content Strategy.md
- 📱 Social Calendar.md
- ✏️ 3 Priority Articles.md
