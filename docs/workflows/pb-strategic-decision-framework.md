## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name and industry
- ❓ The strategic question or decision
- ⚖️ Options being considered and constraints

## What BOB does

1. business-baseline → establish current state and context
2. Review baseline — frame the strategic question precisely
3. growth-scaling-playbook → diagnose growth stage and constraint
4. ask-zeus → strategic analysis with first-principles thinking
5. decision-frameworks → apply structured decision framework
6. ask-boardroom → simulate board of advisors debate and vote

## Outputs

- 📊 Growth stage diagnosis
- 🧠 First-principles strategic analysis
- 📐 Structured decision framework
- 👥 Board of advisors recommendation

## Benefits

- 3× faster
- Multiple expert viewpoints in parallel
- Decision documented — not just remembered
