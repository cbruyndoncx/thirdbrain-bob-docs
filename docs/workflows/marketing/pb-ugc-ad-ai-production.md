# UGC Ad AI Production

*Platform-ready UGC ad variants with hook-tested script, AI-generated scenes, captions, and CTA — ready for paid launch*

> You need ten ad variants.
> A shoot gets you one, in three weeks.

- **Area:** Marketing
- **Time savings:** Typical run: 2-3 hours across 2 sessions
- **Try it:** "Need to produce a UGC-style video ad for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 2 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per trigger event

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → verify ICP, product brief, brand voice, and platform are all defined before proceeding
2. icp-discovery → confirm target audience: pain point, exact language they use, scroll behaviour, platform preference
3. HUMAN review → select the primary hook for v1; confirm Hook B and C for v2/v3; approve script direction before writing begins
4. HUMAN review → watch all 3 variants end-to-end; confirm hooks are distinct; approve for launch
