# Market-to-Order

*Market analyzed, pricing set, campaigns running, and qualified orders flowing in*

> Demand is out there.
> But nothing turns a signal into an order.

- **Area:** Marketing
- **Time savings:** Typical run: Weeks — ongoing cycle of analysis, pricing, and campaign execution
- **Try it:** "Need to turn market demand signals into qualified orders — pricing, campaigns, demand generation"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 6 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per trigger event
- **Published:** 2026-08-01

## Inputs

- 📦 Which products or services to drive demand for
- 📋 Current market conditions or seasonal context
- 💰 Marketing budget available
- 📋 Historical performance data (if available)
- 💰 Pricing flexibility and margin targets

## What BOB does

1. analytics-tracking → review market signals: website traffic, search trends, seasonal patterns, competitor moves
2. ga4-analytics → analyze conversion data, identify high-intent segments, map demand patterns
3. pricing-strategy → review pricing vs market, analyze margins, recommend adjustments
4. Review pricing recommendations and approve changes
5. icp-discovery → refine target audience based on demand data
6. paid-ads → design campaigns targeting high-intent segments, set budgets and bids
7. ab-test-setup → test campaign variants, measure conversion to qualified orders

## Outputs

- 📊 Signals Report.md
- 💰 Pricing Recs.md
- 🎯 ICP Segments.md
- 📋 Live Campaigns.md
- 📋 Pipeline Tracker.md
