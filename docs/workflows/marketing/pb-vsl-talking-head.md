> You need a sales video.
> But production takes weeks and costs thousands.

**Outcome:** Script, talking head, B-roll, captions — landing-page-ready VSL.

- **Area:** Marketing
- **Time savings:** ~12 hrs production → ~3 hrs automated (4× faster)
- **Try it:** "Need a video sales letter with a person presenting"
- **Compounding:** Avatar and brand assets reusable — each VSL is faster to produce
- **Frequency:** Per product/offer launch

## Inputs

- 🎯 Product/offer being sold
- 📸 Presenter photo (front-facing, high-res)
- ⏱️ Target video length (60-120s)

## What BOB does

1. Define VSL objective, target audience, and CTA
2. marketing-video-guide → write VSL script (PAS formula)
3. copy-editing → clarity and persuasion sweep
4. Review and approve script
5. avatar-video → generate talking head from photo
6. veo-video-gen → generate B-roll scenes
7. Review talking head and B-roll clips
8. process-video → assemble final cut with captions
9. Review final cut, request revisions
10. content-scorer → score persuasion and CTA strength

## Outputs

- 📝 PAS-formula VSL script
- 🎬 Talking head video with B-roll
- 🎥 Final assembled VSL with captions
- 📊 Content score report

## Benefits

- 4× faster
- Professional quality without a production team
- Iterate versions without reshooting
