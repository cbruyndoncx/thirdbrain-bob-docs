# Daily Content Machine — how BOB researches & scripts a video before you record

*A morning content brief (top trend angle + full script + 3 thumbnail concepts) delivered daily and unattended, stopping at the point where you record*

> Every morning, the same question.
> What should I post today?

- **Area:** Marketing
- **Time savings:** ~2 hrs trend-hunting + scripting → 10-20 min unattended
- **Try it:** "Every morning — today's best content opportunity, scripted and thumbnailed"
- **Compounding:** Each day's brief sharpens your trend signal and voice templates
- **Frequency:** Daily, unattended (06:00)
- **Published:** 2026-06-29

## Inputs

- 🎯 youtube-context.md
- 🗣️ tone_of_voice.md
- 📡 content-trends domain

## What BOB does

1. continuous-research → scout & score trend outliers
2. yt-idea-generation → pick top angle + 5 hooks
3. yt-script-structure → full script in brand voice
4. yt-hook-writing → craft & prepend the hook
5. yt-thumbnail → 3 on-brand thumbnail concepts
6. daily-briefing → assemble the morning brief
7. You record the video

## Outputs

- 📊 scored-outliers.md
- 💡 angle+hooks.md
- 📝 script.md
- 🖼️ thumb-1..3.png
- 📋 content-brief.md
