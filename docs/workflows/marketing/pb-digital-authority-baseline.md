- **Frequency:** Per new engagement or bi-annually

## Inputs

- 🌐 Client website URL and social profiles
- 🏢 Client name and industry
- 🔑 Key competitors and target keywords

## What BOB does

1. icp-discovery → define target audience for positioning
2. website-audit → full website analysis (SEO + GEO)
3. social-media-audit → assess social presence
4. Review audits — prioritize channels and gaps
5. seo-audit → deep technical and on-page SEO
6. geo-audit → AI visibility and citation audit
7. marketing-report → merge all into scored report with PDF

## Outputs

- 👤 Target audience ICP
- 🔎 Website, SEO, social, and GEO audits
- 📊 Scored marketing report with PDF
- 📋 Prioritized action plan
