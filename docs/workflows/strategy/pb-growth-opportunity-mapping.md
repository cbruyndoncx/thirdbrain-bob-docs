# Growth ideas everywhere.

*Prioritized growth opportunities with market sizing and roadmap*

> Growth ideas everywhere.
> No way to know which ones are worth pursuing.

**Outcome:** Opportunities sized, prioritised, and mapped to a roadmap — not a wish list.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Time savings:** ~10 hrs manual → ~2.5 hrs automated (4× faster)
- **Try it:** "Client wants to identify next growth lever"
- **Compounding:** Growth data and scenario models become strategic planning infrastructure
- **Frequency:** Quarterly or per strategic review
- **Published:** 2026-03-30

## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name and industry
- 📈 Current annual revenue and growth rate
- 💡 Top 3 growth hypotheses

## What BOB does

1. business-baseline → map business model and growth constraints
2. Review baseline — confirm growth stage and constraint
3. growth-scaling-playbook → diagnose growth stage and next moves
4. market-sizing-analysis → size top opportunities (TAM/SAM/SOM)
5. strategy-frameworks → build strategic roadmap
6. business-reporting → create growth forecast and scenarios

## Outputs

- 🔍 Growth stage diagnosis
- 📊 TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing
- 🗺️ Strategic roadmap with 90-day plan
- 📈 Growth forecast with scenarios

## Benefits

- 4× faster
- Market sizing grounds every opportunity
- Prioritisation removes gut-feel decisions
