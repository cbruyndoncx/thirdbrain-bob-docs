> Stuck on a strategic decision.
> Wishing you had advisors. But you're on your own.

**Outcome:** Real-world thought leaders debate your question — with votes, rebuttals, and a decision brief.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Level:** beginner
- **Roles:** CEO / Founder
- **Time savings:** ~8 hrs deliberation → ~15 min automated (32× faster)
- **Try it:** "Ask the board: Should we raise prices by 20%?"
- **Compounding:** Decision history builds institutional memory — pattern recognition improves over time
- **Frequency:** Per major strategic decision
- **Published:** 2026-03-26

## Inputs

- ❓ Strategic question
- 🏢 Business context (profile dir)
- 👥 Board configuration

## What BOB does

1. Confirming question and loading business context
2. Spawning parallel advisor agents (Round 1 positions)
3. Collecting position papers with votes and projections
4. Spawning parallel advisors (Round 2 rebuttals)
5. Generating deliverables (Markdown, HTML, PDF)

## Outputs

- 📊 Board Decision.md
- 🌐 Interactive HTML (with sliders)
- 📕 Board Decision.pdf
- 🗳️ Vote Tracker + Rebuttals

## Benefits

- 32× faster
- Multiple expert perspectives in parallel
- Structured debate — not group-think
