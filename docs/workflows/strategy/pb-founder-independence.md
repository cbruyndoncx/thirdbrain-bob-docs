> You can't take a week off.
> Because you ARE the business.

**Outcome:** Delegation framework built, roles clarified, processes handed off — systematically.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Time savings:** ~10 hrs manual → ~2.5 hrs automated (4× faster)
- **Try it:** "Founder is the bottleneck — business depends on one person"
- **Compounding:** Each delegation creates capacity for the next — exponential time recovery
- **Frequency:** Per scaling milestone
- **Published:** 2026-03-30

## Inputs

- 👤 Founder's top time-consuming activities
- 👥 Current team members and roles
- 🏢 Revenue level and growth target

## What BOB does

1. business-baseline → map founder touchpoints and dependencies
2. Review dependency map — prioritize what to delegate
3. d2-diagram → visualize current vs target org flows
4. technical-writing → create SOPs and decision trees
5. hr-ops → draft role descriptions for expanded positions
6. project-raci → build RACI matrix for key activities

## Outputs

- 🗺️ Founder dependency map
- 📐 Current vs target org flow diagrams
- 📋 SOPs and decision trees
- 📊 RACI matrix for key activities

## Benefits

- 4× faster
- Role clarity removes bottleneck confusion
- Process handoffs that actually stick
