**Outcome:** QBR with performance trends, ROI evidence, and a next-quarter action plan.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Time savings:** ~6 hrs manual QBR prep → ~2 hrs automated (3× faster)
- **Try it:** "End of quarter — prepare the QBR"
- **Compounding:** Each QBR builds on prior baselines — trend visibility improves every quarter
- **Frequency:** Quarterly per client
- **Published:** 2026-03-31

## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name
- 📅 Quarter being reviewed (e.g. Q1 2026)
- 💡 Key wins and challenges to highlight

## What BOB does

1. ai-work-audit → aggregate sessions and deliverables for quarter
2. business-reporting → financial cohort analysis and ROI
3. Review metrics — confirm accuracy, add business context
4. marketing-report → compare scores vs prior quarter baselines
5. exec-comms → draft QBR executive summary (SCQA)
6. Review QBR, personalize next-quarter recommendations

## Outputs

- 📊 Quarter session and deliverable metrics
- 💰 Financial ROI analysis
- 📈 Score comparison vs prior baselines
- 📄 QBR executive summary

## Benefits

- 3× faster
- Trends visible across quarters
- Next-quarter plan built into the review
