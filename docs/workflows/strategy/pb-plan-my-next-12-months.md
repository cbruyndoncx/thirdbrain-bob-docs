# Plan My Next 12 Months

*12-Month Business Operating Plan with OKRs, milestones, financial model, market sizing, and a decision filter*

<div class="video-wrap"><iframe width="100%" height="420" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/X8zskLUqJAE?rel=0" title="Plan My Next 12 Months" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"></iframe></div>

> A year is twelve months.
> Most plans stop being real by March.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Time savings:** Typical run: 3-5 hours, multi-session
- **Try it:** "Business owner wants a 12-month operating plan with strategic direction, OKRs, financial projections, and market context"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 8 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per trigger event
- **Published:** 2026-08-01

## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name and industry
- 📋 Current annual revenue and growth rate
- 🏢 Where the business should be in 12 months (vision)
- 💰 Top 3 constraints (time, budget, team, market, etc.)
- 📋 Key strategic questions or decisions pending

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → verify client context completeness and flag blocking gaps before proceeding
2. business-baseline → map current business model, growth constraints, and strategic position
3. Review baseline — confirm current state, define 12-month vision, and declare top 3 constraints
4. strategy-frameworks → define strategic direction with where-to-play and how-to-win choices
5. market-sizing-analysis → size target market opportunities with TAM/SAM/SOM
6. startup-financial-modeling → build 12-month financial model with base, optimistic, and conservative scenarios
7. okr-frameworks → set annual OKRs with quarterly milestones and a decision filter for saying yes/no
8. business-reporting → consolidate all outputs into a single 12-Month Business Operating Plan
9. pdf-report → generate branded PDF of the 12-Month Business Operating Plan with strategy, financials, OKRs, and market sizing

## Outputs

- 🗺️ 12-Month Plan.md
- 📋 Annual OKRs.md
- 💰 Financial Model.xlsx
- 📋 TAM-SAM-SOM.md
- 📋 Decision Filter.md
- 📊 Branded Report.pdf
