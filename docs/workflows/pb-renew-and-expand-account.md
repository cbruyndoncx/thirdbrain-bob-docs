## Inputs

- 🏢 Client name and `client-id`
- 📋 Contract / renewal timing or milestone timing
- 📋 Current service scope
- 📝 Known renewal risk or expansion opportunity, if any
- 📋 Revenue at stake and strategic importance

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → verify client context completeness and confirm there is enough commercial context to make a continuity decision
2. client-health-tracker → produce current health snapshot, including at-risk and expansion signals
3. retention-check → prepare the renewal-readiness brief: value delivered, satisfaction pattern, renewal recommendation, and explicit risks
4. Review readiness — decide whether the account is currently in one of three states: `renew`, `renew-expand`, or `non-renew-risk`
5. client-ascension → if state is `renew` or `renew-expand`, produce the continuity path: ascension map, renewal / expansion script, and recommended commercial structure
6. Review continuity path — decide the commercial ask: renew as-is, renew with expansion, restructure, or hold
7. Conduct the renewal / expansion conversation or prepare the package for the account owner
8. meeting-intelligence → debrief the continuity conversation: objections, stakeholder signals, pricing pressure, and next-step commitments

## Outputs

- 📋 Renewal Readiness Brief.md
- 📁 Expansion Path Recommendation.md
- 📝 Renewal / Expansion Conversation Script.md
- 📋 Decision Log.md
- 📋 If no-renew: Offboarding Branch.md
