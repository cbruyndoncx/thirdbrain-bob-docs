# Grow My Revenue

*Diagnosed growth constraint with targeted 90-day action plan spanning pricing, acquisition, conversion, or product-led growth*

> Revenue plateaued.
> And there are twenty levers you could pull.

- **Area:** Sales & Clients
- **Time savings:** Typical run: 4-6 hours, multi-session
- **Try it:** "Business owner wants to grow revenue but doesn't know which lever to pull, or revenue has plateaued"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 6 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per trigger event
- **Published:** 2026-08-01

## Inputs

- 🏢 Business name and industry
- 📋 Current annual revenue and growth rate
- 📋 Top 3 revenue concerns (in the owner's own words)
- 📋 Team size and available capacity
- 💰 Budget for growth initiatives (if any)
- 📋 Current sales/marketing channels
- 📋 Biggest recent win and biggest recent loss

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → verify client context completeness and flag blocking gaps before proceeding
2. icp-discovery → define or refine ideal customer profile for primary revenue stream
3. product-marketing-context → establish positioning, messaging, and competitive landscape
4. Review ICP and positioning — confirm accuracy before diagnosis
5. growth-scaling-playbook → diagnose growth stage, identify binding constraint, and recommend 90-day focus
6. Review diagnosis — confirm binding constraint and select 1-2 routes below to maintain focus
7. Integrate outputs into 90-day action plan with 3-5 priorities, owners, deadlines, and success metrics — update crm-pipeline with any new deals or stage changes
8. pdf-report → generate branded PDF of the 90-day action plan with diagnosis summary, route outputs, and priorities

## Outputs

- 🎯 ICP Profile.md
- 📋 Growth Diagnosis.md
- 📋 Route Deliverables.md
- 🗺️ 90-Day Plan.md
- 📊 Branded Report.pdf
