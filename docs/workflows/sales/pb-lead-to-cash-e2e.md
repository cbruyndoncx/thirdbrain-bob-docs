# Lead-to-Cash E2E

*Lead converted, deal closed, work delivered, invoice paid, and margin reviewed*

<div class="video-wrap"><iframe width="100%" height="420" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/88lrnBeUa1U?rel=0" title="Lead-to-Cash E2E" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"></iframe></div>

> A lead came in.
> Somewhere between there and the invoice, it stalls.

- **Area:** Sales & Clients
- **Time savings:** Typical run: Weeks to months — runs across the full sales and delivery cycle
- **Try it:** "New lead or prospect enters the pipeline — orchestrate the full journey to cash collected"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 7 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per opportunity
- **Published:** 2026-08-01

## Inputs

- 👤 Lead source and initial contact information
- 📦 Product or service being sold
- 📋 Deal value estimate
- 📅 Timeline expectations
- 📋 Delivery scope (once deal closes)

## What BOB does

1. icp-discovery + lead-qualifier → identify if prospect matches ICP, score with BANT/MEDDIC
2. Review qualification and decide: pursue, nurture, or disqualify
3. research-company + prospect-audit → deep prospect research, score company fit
4. decision-maker-mapper → map buying committee and identify champion
5. proposal-writer → draft proposal from meeting notes and client intelligence
6. Review proposal, adjust pricing, send to prospect
7. create-client → on deal won, set up client workspace with profile, contacts, project folders
8. invoice-description → generate invoice line items from completed work
9. Review and send invoice, track payment
10. ar-followup → follow up on overdue invoices, escalate as needed

## Outputs

- 📊 Lead Scorecard.md
- 📋 Buying Committee.md
- 📋 Won Deal Record.md
- 🏢 Client Workspace
- 📄 Invoice.pdf
- 📋 AR Follow-up Log.md
