# Procurement To Signature

*Procurement, security, legal, and signature path made explicit, with owners, blockers, and deadlines tracked to completion*

> The buyer said yes.
> Procurement, security, and legal have not.

- **Area:** Sales & Clients
- **Time savings:** Typical run: 45-120 minutes to establish; ongoing updates through late-stage deal execution
- **Try it:** "The buyer wants to move forward, but procurement, security, legal, or signature process now controls the timeline"
- **Compounding:** Each run refines templates and context — 6 automated steps improve with accumulated data
- **Frequency:** Per opportunity

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → confirm the deal, latest notes, and commercial context are loaded before running paper-process execution
2. MEDDPICC Qualification → extract paper process, decision process, procurement assumptions, and known blockers
3. decision-maker-mapper → confirm procurement, legal, and technical/security reviewers plus signer path where possible
4. negotiation → handle active commercial trade-offs that emerge during procurement or legal review without letting the paper process hide margin concessions
5. Update [[Mutual Action Plan]] with procurement, security, legal, and signature milestones so the close path remains visible
6. crm-pipeline → sync the highest-priority next action and due date back to the deal record

## Outputs

- 📁 Procurement Path.md
- 📋 Security Review Tracker.md
- 📋 Legal Review Tracker.md
- 📋 Signature Readiness Checklist.md
