## Inputs

- 🔍 Blog topic or target keyword
- 🏢 Business goal (traffic, leads, authority, sales support)
- 🎯 Target audience
- 📋 Funnel stage (awareness, consideration, conversion)
- 🎙️ CTA goal (book call, contact form, download, etc.)
- 🏢 Scope: company or client-id

## What BOB does

1. vault-preflight → verify client/company context completeness and flag blocking gaps
2. seo-content-machine → research keywords + write article
3. content-scorer → scrub AI patterns and score content quality
4. content-scorer (blog-business-audit) → audit blog as a business asset, full mode
5. Review audit report — decide: publish, polish, or rewrite
6. seo-content-machine (rewrite-from-audit) → rewrite targeting audit weaknesses, skip if score >= 7.5
7. content-scorer (blog-business-audit) → re-audit rewritten blog, confirm score improved
8. Review final draft and approve
9. social-content → LinkedIn + X posts from final article
10. wordpress-publisher → publish article

## Outputs

- ✏️ Blog Article.md
- 📊 Quality Scorecard.md
- 📊 Audit Report.md
- 📱 Social Posts.md
- 🌐 Published URL
