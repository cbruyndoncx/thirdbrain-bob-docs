# culture-craft

Use this skill to turn culture intent into observable behaviors, rituals, and hiring standards rather than vague value statements.

- **Area:** People
- **Category:** human resources
- **Level:** beginner
- **Input:** conversation
- **Output:** document
- **Setup:** none
- **Roles:** CEO / Founder, HR Manager

Designs team culture, defines core values, and creates reinforcing rituals and excellence standards.

## How you ask

- “Define core values for our engineering team”
- “Design team rituals that reinforce our culture”
- “Create excellence standards for our product team”
- “Build a culture document for our growing startup”

## Inputs

- request — What to produce
- scope — `company`, or a client-id under {company.clients}/ — a string with one reserved value, not a closed set

## Outputs

- culture-craft (markdown)
- {scope.knowledge-base.product}/culture-craft-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md
