# demand-signal-analysis

Analyze market demand signals to surface opportunities and flag risks before they hit revenue. Covers real-time trend scanning, seasonal demand mapping, and competitor move monitoring — three distinct reads that feed planning, campaign timing, and pricing decisions.

Analyzes qualitative market signals from search trends, competitor moves, channel performance, and emerging demand opportunities.

## How you ask

- “Market shift”
- “New demand opportunity”
- “Competitor move”

## Inputs

- topic — Product, category, keyword cluster, or market segment to scan
- timeframe — Lookback window (e.g., 90 days, 6 months). Defaults to 90 days.
- channels — Which signal sources to prioritize (search, social, sales data, industry reports)
- topic — Product, category, or service to map
- years — Historical years to consider. Defaults to 2–3 years.
- competitors — Competitor names or domains to watch
- category — Product or service category in scope

## Outputs

- competitor-watch-report (markdown)
- prefer a permanent directory)
- seasonal-map (markdown)
- trend-scan-report (markdown)
