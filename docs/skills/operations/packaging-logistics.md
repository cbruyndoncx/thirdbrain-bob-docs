# packaging-logistics

Generate accurate packing specifications, pre-shipment checklists, and reusable packaging standards without WMS or ERP integration — driven by SME input and product knowledge.

- **Area:** Operations
- **Category:** operations
- **Level:** beginner
- **Input:** conversation
- **Output:** document
- **Setup:** none
- **Roles:** Operations Manager

Generates packing specifications, label and marking requirements, packaging checklists, and shipping preparation documents.

## How you ask

- “Packaging specs”
- “Shipping labels”
- “Packaging checklist”

## Inputs

- product — Product name or SKU
- destination_type — Domestic / Export / Retail / E-commerce (domestic | export | retail | ecommerce)
- order_ref — Order reference or shipment identifier
- product_line — Product line or category being standardised

## Outputs

- packaging-standards (markdown)
- packing-spec (markdown)
- prefer a permanent directory)
- shipping-prep-doc (markdown)
