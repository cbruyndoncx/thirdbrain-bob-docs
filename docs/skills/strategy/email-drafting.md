# email-drafting

Write clear, purposeful professional emails. One email, one purpose, no filler. Covers client correspondence, introductions, follow-ups, meeting requests, internal updates, thank-yous, and apology/issue notifications.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Category:** business
- **Level:** beginner
- **Input:** conversation
- **Output:** document
- **Setup:** none
- **Roles:** Head of Sales

Draft individual professional emails — client replies, introductions, follow-ups, proposal covers, meeting requests, internal updates, thank-yous, apologies, and issue…

## How you ask

- “Draft a concise reply confirming the revised delivery date and asking for approval by Friday.”
- “Write an introduction email between our implementation partner and the client sponsor.”
- “Turn these meeting notes into one follow-up email with a single next step.”
- “Draft an apology email that acknowledges the delay without over-explaining it.”

## Inputs

- request — What you need to communicate — recipient, purpose, key points, and any tone preferences
- thread — Previous email thread if replying
- tone_of_voice — Brand voice context for tone calibration

## Outputs

- Ready-to-send email with subject line, body, and sign-off
- conversation
