# strategic-pm

Reframe tactical work into a strategic brief with a real bet, a named trade-off, an outcome, and a time horizon.

- **Area:** Strategy
- **Category:** product management
- **Level:** beginner
- **Input:** conversation
- **Output:** document
- **Setup:** none
- **Roles:** Product Manager

Creates strategic framing briefs in Obsidian that tie initiatives to outcomes, trade-offs, and now-next-later horizons.

## How you ask

- “Help me be more strategic about our roadmap priorities”
- “Create a strategic framing brief for this initiative”
- “Tie our feature work to business outcomes”
- “Map our initiatives to now-next-later horizons”

## Inputs

- request — What to produce
- scope — `company`, or a client-id under {company.clients}/ — a string with one reserved value, not a closed set

## Outputs

- strategic-pm (markdown)
- {scope.knowledge-base.product}/strategic-pm-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md
