# 5. Core Concepts, Made Simple

A few ideas make everything else click. None of them are technical — they're just how BOB thinks.

## The vault is your company's brain

Everything BOB knows about your business lives in the vault: plain, readable files on your own machine, organised into a stable structure — clients, company divisions, resources, personal notes. This has three consequences that matter:

1. **No lock-in.** Your data is files. You can read, move, back up, or leave with them at any time.
2. **Context compounds.** Every meeting note, decision, and deliverable you keep makes the next answer sharper.
3. **One fact, one home.** Each kind of information has exactly one place to live, so nothing is lost and nothing is duplicated. BOB routes new notes to the right place automatically.

## Skills are workflows, not prompts

A skill is a written procedure: what inputs it needs, what steps it takes, what checks it applies, what "done" looks like. Because the procedure is written down, results are **repeatable** — the tenth proposal follows the same quality bar as the first, and improvements to a skill benefit every future run.

## Playbooks chain skills into processes

Where a skill is one job, a playbook is a **process** — onboarding a client, closing a month, running a weekly review. Playbooks execute step by step with checkpoints, and each run is logged: what was invoked, with what input, producing which files. You can retrace what BOB did after the fact.

## The rules BOB never breaks

| Rule | What it means for you |
|------|-----------------------|
| 1. Your source material is sacred | Files you wrote are never silently overwritten or deleted |
| 2. Nothing risky without a "go" | Sending, publishing, deleting, paying — anything hard to undo waits for your explicit approval. Drafts flow freely. |
| 3. Big builds get a plan first | For anything beyond routine, BOB shows you a plan to approve before starting |

**The short version:** BOB works draft-first. It creates freely, but you are always the one who presses "send".

## You choose the AI model

The model is layer 4 — swappable without touching your vault, skills, or habits.

| Choose | When |
|--------|------|
| Claude (default) | Best all-round quality for business reasoning and writing |
| Lighter/cheaper variants | Simple drafts and routine work where cost matters |
| A local model | Content whose AI processing must stay on your machine. It does not cover skills that call outside services or skills fetched from the skills server (see [chapter 19](19-data-safety.md)) |

## BOB gets better as your vault matures

- **Week 1:** competent but generic answers; more clarifying questions
- **Month 1:** answers tailored to your business — profile, clients, and processes are in place
- **Month 3+:** BOB references your past decisions, prior client work, and your writing style

The vault is a flywheel: the more you work in it, the smarter every future answer becomes.
