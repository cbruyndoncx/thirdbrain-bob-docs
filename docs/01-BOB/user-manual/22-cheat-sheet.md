# 22. One-Page Cheat Sheet

Print this page and pin it by your desk. It's the whole system at a glance.

## The core loop

**Ask in plain English → BOB picks a skill → review the draft → approve.** You are always the final approver.

## Every day & week

| Say | You get |
|-----|---------|
| "Daily briefing" | Today's focus: tasks, follow-ups, what needs attention |
| "Run my weekly review" | Last week reviewed, this week planned |
| "/bob-doctor" | Health check when something feels off |

## Get work done

| Say | Area |
|-----|------|
| "Draft a proposal for ___" / "score this lead" | Sales |
| "Write a LinkedIn post from ___" / "repurpose this" | Marketing |
| "Chase the open invoices" / "close the month" | Finance |
| "Summarise this meeting: decisions and actions" | Operations |
| "Act as my board: should I ___?" | Strategy |

## Remember & find

| Say | You get |
|-----|---------|
| "Save this as a note in the right place" | Filed where it belongs, linked |
| "What did we decide about ___?" | An answer from your own vault |
| "What skills do you have for ___?" | Routing help, or browse [SKILLS](../../skills/index.md) |

## The three things to remember

1. **Nothing sends without your "go"** — BOB is draft-only by default.
2. **Context in, quality out** — point BOB at the folder that matters.
3. **Forgot a command?** There are none to memorise. Plain words always work.
