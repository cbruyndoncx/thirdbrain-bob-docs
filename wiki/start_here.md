the most from it. Pick the row that fits where you are.

## The usual path

1. **Understand.** [[01-BOB/Onboarding/bob_overview|What is BOB?]] explains the system and the
   four layers it is built from. The [[01-BOB/Onboarding/bob_learning_path|learning path]] gives a
   structured route through the rest.
2. **Install and check.** With a set-up-for-you install, the [[quickstart|Quickstart]] confirms
   that everything works. To install yourself, check your machine with
   [[01-BOB/Onboarding/environment_checklist|Before you install]] and follow the install guides;
   chapter 3 of the manual, [[01-BOB/user-manual/03-getting-started|Getting started]], summarises
   the steps.
3. **Use and grow.** [[using_bob|Using BOB day to day]] covers the everyday patterns. Read the
   concept pages as you meet the ideas in real work, not all at once.

## What is in the documentation

- **Get started**: what BOB is, the quickstart, installation, and first steps in Obsidian.
- **User manual**: the complete manual, chapter by chapter.
- **Guides**: day-to-day use, the workspace modules, and the concepts behind playbooks and skills.
- **Prompts**: ready-made prompts for each area of the business, starting from the
  [[06-Prompt-Showcase/Prompt Showcase|prompt showcase]].
- **Library**: every skill and workflow in the current release.
- **Help**: troubleshooting by symptom, and reference pages.

The documentation is online at <https://cbruyndoncx.github.io/thirdbrain-bob-docs/> and is kept
current between releases. The PDF editions of the User Manual and the Getting Started Guide are in
the [ThirdBrain Tech classroom on Skool](https://www.skool.com/thirdbrain-tech-3102).

## Can't find it?

- **Search**: the search box on the docs site, or `Ctrl+Shift+F` inside Obsidian.
- **Ask BOB**: in the Agent Client panel, ask "find a guide about …".
